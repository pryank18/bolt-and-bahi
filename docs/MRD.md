# Bolt & Bahi — Market Requirements Document

## Market problem

Fabric trading in India — retail counters, wholesale distribution, and job-work units — is still run largely offline: billing on paper, stock tracked by memory or notebook, and GST split calculated by hand on every invoice. This is a large, fragmented market, and the operational pain — invoicing errors, missed reorders, uncollected receivables — scales with the size of the trader's business, yet most traders have no dedicated system, only paper and WhatsApp.

## Target market

Indian textile traders — retail counters, wholesale distributors, and job-work units — particularly those currently running billing and stock manually, without an existing ERP or accounting system. Scoped for single-location and small multi-location traders rather than large textile mills or enterprise distribution chains.

## Why now

Smartphone and browser access is now common even among small traders, making a browser-based, no-install ledger viable where it wasn't before. At the same time, enterprise ERP and accounting software remains priced and scoped for large manufacturers and distributors, leaving a gap for a lightweight, GST-aware ledger built for a single trader or counter.

## Competitive landscape

- Enterprise ERP/accounting platforms — comprehensive but priced and scoped for large manufacturers and multi-location distributors
- Generic billing/invoicing apps — handle GST invoices but don't track roll-level fabric stock, job work, or wholesale price slabs
- Status quo (paper bahi, WhatsApp, spreadsheets) — the default for most traders today; zero software cost but carries the invoicing, stock, and receivables problems Bolt & Bahi is built to remove

## Value proposition

Bolt & Bahi gives a fabric trader their own GST-correct billing and stock ledger — covering retail, wholesale, job work, and dispatch in one place, in their own language — without the cost or complexity of enterprise ERP software, and without giving up control of their data to a cloud account they didn't ask for.

## Positioning

A trader-first ledger for the textile trade segment that enterprise ERP software doesn't reach — positioned as the step up from paper and WhatsApp, not a competitor to large-scale manufacturing ERP suites.

## Status

Live demo at pryank18.github.io/bolt-and-bahi/, running on browser localStorage with a bring-your-own-key Anthropic integration for ARA. No pricing model or go-to-market validation with real traders yet — that follows the next phase, once the app is connected to the verified Supabase schema for real accounts and sync.
